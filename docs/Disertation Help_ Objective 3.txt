OBJECTIVE 3 — Determination of Market Energy Commitment

Objective:
To determine market commitment quantities from next-day solar PV
forecasts while accounting for forecast uncertainty and imbalance costs.

1. Input from Objectives 1 and 2

Use the best-performing forecasting model identified under Objective 1
and select the forecast for a single one-hour delivery interval:
12:00–13:00.

Let:

Ê₁₂ = forecast PV energy for 12:00–13:00 (kWh)

The forecast must be an actual output of the selected model, not an
assumed/hypothetical value.

From Objective 2, obtain the empirical forecast-error distribution for
the corresponding interval:

e_(t) = E_(t) − Ê_(t)

including the relevant residual quantiles.

2. Determine the Market Commitment

Define the economic consequences of the commitment decision as:

C_(u) = cost of under-commitmentC_(o) = cost of over-commitment

where under-commitment represents potential income forgone from
available energy that was not committed, while over-commitment
represents shortfall/imbalance exposure when committed energy exceeds
available generation. This is consistent with the Newsvendor
interpretation already established in the dissertation.

Calculate the Newsvendor critical fractile:

$$\boxed{q^{*} = \frac{C_{u}}{C_{u} + C_{o}}}
$$

Use this critical fractile to select the corresponding quantile from the
forecast distribution:

$$\boxed{B_{12}^{*} = F_{12}^{- 1}\left( q^{*} \right)}
$$

where:

B₁₂^(*) = committed energy for 12:00–13:00 (kWh)

The commitment should therefore be determined from the forecast +
quantified uncertainty + market costs, rather than by applying an
arbitrary fixed percentage to the forecast.

3. Evaluate the Commitment

After determining B₁₂^(*), compare it with the actual measured
Strathmore energy generation for the same 12:00–13:00 interval:

E₁₂^(actual)

Calculate:

Shortfall = max (B₁₂^(*), −E₁₂^(actual)0)Surplus = max (E₁₂^(actual), −B₁₂^(*)0)

The actual generation is used only for ex-post evaluation and must not
be used to determine the commitment.
