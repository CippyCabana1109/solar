Objective 1 genuine test-week evaluation used March Week 1 (2025-03-01 to 2025-03-07) and the fixed daytime window 06:00-18:59, with 91 daytime observations. This is a genuine test-week result, not cross-validation.

Final daytime test-week ranking:

Rank  Model                    MAE (W)  RMSE (W)  sMAPE (%)  R2
1     XGBoost                  16510    29067     27.09      0.920
2     XGBoost-SARIMAX Hybrid   26772    40689     36.30      0.843
3     SARIMAX                  39894    51396     53.55      0.750
4     SARIMAX-XGBoost Hybrid   70032    85511     58.35      0.307
5     Prophet                  94256    97490    112.81      0.099

XGBoost was the overall winner. Prophet's low test-week R2 is a genuine
out-of-sample result: the saved Prophet artifact used the six expected NASA
weather regressors, the test regressors were complete and within the training
ranges, and training and test plant power used the same Watt scale. Prophet
followed the daylight shape but underpredicted the March peak and produced
negative dawn/evening forecasts, which explains its weak test performance.

The two hybrids used XGBoost and SARIMAX, the two best standalone models on
this test week. Their residual correctors were trained only from out-of-fold
validation residuals. Both hybrids underperformed XGBoost on this test week.

March Week 1 plant totals are derived_partial_sum values averaging about 77%
inverter coverage during daytime hours, so results represent best-available
data rather than certified full-plant readings. Inv18 and Inv19 stopped
reporting during February, while Inv24 and Inv25 were absent from the
February export; all four were absent from the March exports. This is a
material limitation of the test data.
