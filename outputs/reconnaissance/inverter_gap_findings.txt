INVERTER DATA GAP FINDINGS

Scope
The February and March 2025 production exports were inspected at their native
5-minute resolution. The confirmed plant-level aggregate columns are not
present in these files; they contain individual inverter channels instead.
No inverter-summing rule has been applied.

Affected channels
- Inv18 and Inv19 were populated in historical AC production exports, with
  the last observed values in the available pre-February/February material at
  2025-02-13 10:00.
- Inv24 was populated only sparsely in historical AC production exports, with
  the last observed value at 2024-10-02 08:15.
- Inv25 was populated only sparsely in historical AC production exports, with
  the last observed value at 2024-09-27 01:20.
- Inv24 and Inv25 are completely empty in the February 2025 production
  exports. Inv18 and Inv19 stop reporting during February and all four
  channels are completely empty in the March Week 1, Week 2, and Week 3
  production exports.

March 2025 completeness
Each March file contains 2,016 five-minute intervals. All 26 channels are
missing at least one value at every timestamp; therefore the all-26-required
option leaves zero usable intervals.

                         Any reported   At least 22   At least 24   All 26
March Week 1 (2,016)           1,094          830           0           0
March Week 2 (2,016)           1,616            0           0           0
March Week 3 (2,016)           1,051            0           0           0

The "any reported" count means at least one inverter power value is populated.
The 22- and 24-channel counts are equal-count diagnostics only, not installed
capacity thresholds. Installed capacity per inverter was not present in the
exports, so a true 90-percent-capacity rule cannot yet be calculated.

Interpretation and limitation
The pattern is consistent with an incomplete or changed production export,
although the plant data manager may confirm that some channels were offline
for maintenance. This is an infrastructure/data-availability limitation, not
a justified reason to silently replace missing inverter values with zero or
to sum partial inverter output. A corrected export or maintenance confirmation
is required before extending the master dataset into February or March 2025.

Current modelling boundary
The provisional master uses only plant-level aggregate production columns for
January 2024 through January 2025. It does not include individual inverter
columns and does not determine the February/March production target.
